Research interests:

I am primarily interested in theoretical and foundational research at the intersection of quantum information, quantum algorithms, quantum information theory,theoretical computer science, and many-body physics

Prioritize papers with rigorous theoretical results, complexity analyses, provable guarantees, lower or upper bounds, new algorithmic techniques, and numerical simulations of the algorithms.

1. Ground-state preparation and quantum state preparation

I am particularly interested in algorithms and theoretical results for preparing ground states of Hamiltonians, including:

* ground-state preparation algorithms and their computational complexity;
* rigorous upper and lower bounds on preparation complexity;
* dependence on spectral gaps, condition numbers, locality, Hamiltonian structure, precision, and system size;
* adiabatic state preparation and related methods;
* variational and non-variational ground-state preparation methods when accompanied by meaningful theoretical guarantees;
* dissipative and measurement-based preparation methods
* quantum algorithms for preparing ground states whose encoded information represents solutions to computational problems.

In particular, prioritize work connecting ground-state preparation to computational problems such as:

* combinatorial optimization;
* constraint satisfaction;
* word problems;
* grammar and language-related computational problems;
* equational reasoning;
* symbolic reasoning;
* compilation and program-related problems;
* coherent quantum sample preparation;
* other computational problems encoded into Hamiltonians or quantum states.

2. Hamiltonian complexity and parent Hamiltonians

I am interested in theoretical methods for:

* constructing parent Hamiltonians;
* identifying or characterizing parent Hamiltonians of quantum states and tensor-network states;
* inverse problems relating quantum states, tensor networks, and Hamiltonians;
* locality, frustration-freeness, spectral properties, and complexity of parent Hamiltonians;
* computational complexity associated with Hamiltonian ground spaces and ground-state preparation.

3. Tensor networks

I am particularly interested in theoretical tensor-network research, including:

* connections between tensor networks, quantum information, quantum algorithms, and theoretical computer science.

4. Quantum algorithms and theoretical computer science

Include quantum algorithms with strong connections to theoretical computer science, quantum complexity, quantum information theory, algorithms, computational complexity, optimization, sampling, and state preparation.

I am especially interested when quantum states, Hamiltonians, or quantum processes encode solutions to computational problems.

5. Quantum algorithms for genomics

Include theoretically interesting quantum algorithms and complexity results for genomics and computational biology, especially:

* quantum algorithms for genome analysis;
* sequence alignment and comparison;
* genome assembly;
* motif and pattern finding;
* sequencing-related computational problems;
* biological sequence search;
* quantum sampling methods relevant to genomics;
* rigorous speedups, complexity analyses, or quantum query/communication complexity results.

6. General relevance criteria

Strongly prioritize papers that introduce:

* new algorithms;
* new complexity results;
* new bounds;
* rigorous proofs;
* lower bounds;
* structural theorems;
* new state-preparation techniques;
* new Hamiltonian constructions;
* meaningful connections between quantum information, theoretical computer science and many-body physics.
